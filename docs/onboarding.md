# SellQuic Platform Onboarding Guide for Support Assistants

Welcome to the team! This guide will walk you through the entire SellQuic platform, from what a customer sees to what a vendor manages. Your goal is to understand every feature to effectively solve customer problems.

---

## **Part 1: The Customer Experience (Storefront)**

This is what a shopper sees and interacts with.

### **1.1. The Storefront Homepage**
- **Hero Banner:** The main image/promotion at the top.
- **Product Grid:** The main list of products for sale.
- **Categories:** How products are grouped.
- **Promo Bar:** A small text banner at the very top for announcements (e.g., "Free delivery!").
- **Header & Footer:** Navigation, logo, social links.

**Key Issues to Solve:**
- "I can't find the store." (Usually a wrong link).
- "Where is the [product category]?"

### **1.2. Product Detail Page**
- **Images & Video:** All visual media for the product.
- **Price:** The selling price. May show a discounted price and a crossed-out original price.
- **Variants:** Options like size, color, etc. Each can have its own price, stock, and image.
- **Stock Status:** "In Stock", "Out of Stock", or a specific quantity ("Only 5 left!").
- **AI Description:** The descriptive text about the product.
- **"Add to Cart" / "Buy Now":** Core action buttons.

**Key Issues to Solve:**
- "What sizes/colors do you have?"
- "Is this item available?"
- "The price is different when I select an option."

### **1.3. Cart & Checkout**
- **Cart Sheet:** A slide-out summary of items added.
- **Checkout Page:** Where the customer enters their `Name`, `Phone`, and `Address`.
- **Delivery Options:** A list of delivery/pickup locations and their fees.
- **Payment Methods:**
    - **Automated (Paystack):** Pay with Card or Mobile Money instantly.
    - **Manual:** MoMo or Bank Transfer details are displayed for the customer to pay directly.
    - **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Pay upon receiving the item.

**Key Issues to Solve:**
- "How do I pay?"
- "My payment failed."
- "I can't see the delivery option for my area."
- "How much is delivery to [location]?"

### **1.4. Post-Order Experience**
- **Confirmation Page:** Shows order details and payment instructions (for manual payments).
- **Order Tracking:** Customers get a link to a page showing the status of their order (`Awaiting Payment`, `Confirmed`, `Fulfilled`).

**Key Issues to Solve:**
- "I paid but my order is still 'Awaiting Payment'." (Requires manual confirmation by vendor).
- "Where is my order?" (Guide them to check the tracking link or status).

## **Part 2: The Vendor Experience (Dashboard)**

This is the control panel where sellers manage their business.

### **2.1. Core Setup**
- **Products:** Adding/editing products, descriptions, images, prices, stock, and variants.
- **Categories:** Grouping products together.
- **Payments:**
    - **Automated:** Connecting their Paystack account for instant card/MoMo payments.
    - **Manual:** Adding their own MoMo or Bank details to display to customers.
- **Deliveries:** Creating delivery zones and setting fees (e.g., "Accra - GHS 20", "Kumasi - GHS 30").

**Common Vendor Questions:**
- "How do I add a new product?"
- "How do I get paid automatically?"
- "My customer can't see delivery options."

### **2.2. Day-to-Day Operations**
- **Dashboard Homepage:** An overview of revenue, sales, visitors, and recent orders.
- **Orders Page:** A list of all incoming orders. Vendors can update the status (`Confirm Payment`, `Mark as Fulfilled`).

**Common Vendor Questions:**
- "Where do I see my new orders?"
- "How do I confirm I have received payment?"

### **2.3. Marketing & Growth Tools**
- **Discounts & Sales:** Creating site-wide sales (e.g., "20% off everything").
- **Urgency Tools:** Setting up countdown timers for flash sales or cart reservations (Enterprise plan).
- **Abandoned Carts:** Viewing a list of customers who started checkout but didn't finish (Premium plan).
- **Tracking & SEO:** Adding a Facebook Pixel or Google Analytics ID to track ad performance and improve search visibility.

**Common Vendor Questions:**
- "How do I run a sale?"
- "How can I see who didn't finish their order?"

### **2.4. AI-Powered Features**
- **AI Product Descriptions:** A button in the "Add Product" page to auto-generate descriptions.
- **AI Shop Assistant:** The chatbot on the storefront that answers questions and helps customers order.
- **AI Insights:** An analytics page that shows what customers are asking about, product demand, and reasons for lost sales.

**Common Vendor Questions:**
- "How does the AI writer work?"
- "Why did the AI assistant hand a chat over to me?"

### **2.5. Store Customization**
- **Appearance:** Changing the store logo, brand color, and adding social media links.
- **Themes:** Choosing a visual style for the store (e.g., "Classic Light", "Onyx Dark").
- **Domain:** Buying a new domain (`yourbrand.com`) or connecting an existing one.

**Common Vendor Questions:**
- "How do I change my store's color?"
- "Can I use my own domain name?"

### **2.6. Account Management**
- **Subscription:** Upgrading from Free to Premium or Business plans.
- **My Profile:** Updating personal details like name and contact number.
- **Referrals & Affiliates:** Programs for earning rewards by referring other vendors.

**Common Vendor Questions:**
- "What is the difference between the plans?"
- "How do I earn by referring others?"

---

## **Part 3: Your Role as an Assistant**

- **First-line Support:** Your main goal is to solve the customer's problem directly using your platform knowledge.
- **Troubleshooting:** When a feature isn't working as expected, identify if it's a user error (e.g., wrong setting) or a potential bug.
- **Escalation:** If you confirm a bug or cannot solve the problem, your job is to gather all the necessary details (vendor's store name, customer's order ID, screenshots, a clear description of the problem) and escalate it to the technical team.
- **Tone of Voice:** Always be friendly, patient, and empathetic. Our vendors are running businesses, and their time is valuable. Be clear, concise, and professional.
